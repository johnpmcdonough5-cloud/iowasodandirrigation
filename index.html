The Science, Simply

How Spray Foam Keeps San Diego Homes Cool

Traditional insulation only slows heat down. Spray foam does more — it expands to seal the whole building envelope, so the sun's heat stops at the roofline instead of baking your living space.

  • Seals the envelope. Foam fills every gap and crack, stopping the air leaks that waste your AC's effort.
  • Blocks radiant heat. A sealed, foamed roofline keeps a 130°F attic from radiating down into your rooms.
  • Controls moisture. Closed-cell foam resists our coastal humidity, protecting against mold and rot.

Good to Know

Spray Foam Questions, Answered

How much does spray foam insulation cost in San Diego?

Most San Diego spray foam projects run about $1.50–$4.50 per board foot, depending on open-cell vs. closed-cell foam, the thickness required, and access. We always provide a free, no-obligation written estimate so you know the exact number before any work begins.

Is spray foam worth it in San Diego's mild climate?

Absolutely. Inland San Diego regularly hits the 90s–100s in summer, while coastal homes battle humidity and marine moisture. Spray foam creates an air-tight thermal barrier that keeps conditioned air in, blocks outdoor moisture, and reduces cooling costs year-round.

What's the difference between open-cell and closed-cell foam?

Open-cell is softer, expands more, and is a cost-effective choice for interior walls and attics where sound-dampening matters. Closed-cell is denser, adds structural rigidity, delivers a higher R-value per inch, and resists moisture — ideal for crawl spaces and exterior applications. We'll recommend the right one for your project and budget.

How long does the installation take?

Most residential attic or wall jobs are finished in a single day. Larger homes and new-construction projects may take two to three days. You'll get an accurate timeline with your estimate.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — Cali-Cool Spray Foam is a fully licensed and insured California contractor (CSLB Lic. #1234567). Our crews are trained and certified in safe spray foam application, and we carry full liability and workers' comp coverage.
