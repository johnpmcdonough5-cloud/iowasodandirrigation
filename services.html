Two Foams, One Goal

Open-Cell vs. Closed-Cell Spray Foam

Not sure which you need? We'll walk you through it at your free estimate. Here's the short version.

Open-Cell Foam

A lighter, softer foam that expands to fill every gap. Excellent for sound-dampening and a budget-friendly choice for interior walls and attics.

  • R-value ≈ 3.5–3.8 per inch
  • Superior noise reduction
  • Great value for large areas

Closed-Cell Foam

A dense, rigid foam that adds structural strength and acts as its own moisture and air barrier. The premium choice for crawl spaces, roofs, and coastal humidity.

  • R-value ≈ 6.5–7 per inch
  • Blocks moisture & adds rigidity
  • Highest performance per inch
Residential

Attic Spray Foam Insulation

Your attic is the number-one place San Diego homes gain and lose heat. Sealing the roofline or attic floor with spray foam keeps summer heat out and conditioned air where you want it.

  • Stops radiant heat from a 130°F attic
  • Lets your AC finally keep up upstairs
  • Seals gaps that let in dust and allergens
