Our Story

Built in San Diego, for San Diego

Cali-Cool Spray Foam grew out of a simple frustration: too many homeowners were paying for insulation that never solved the problem. Hot upstairs bedrooms, sky-high summer bills, drafty rooms — the same complaints, over and over.

We believed spray foam, applied correctly by people who care, could fix all of it. So we built a small, focused crew that does one thing exceptionally well and treats every home with respect.

Today we're proud to be one of San Diego County's most trusted spray foam contractors — and we've stayed small on purpose, so you always get the owner's attention, not a call center.
